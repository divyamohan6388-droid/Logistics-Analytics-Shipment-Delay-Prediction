# Phase 1 — Project Definition

## 1. Logistics Scenario

The project simulates the role of a Logistics Data Analyst
working for a large e-commerce company that manages thousands
of orders across multiple geographic regions, product categories,
customer segments, and shipping modes.

The company has observed variations in shipment performance,
including delayed shipments. These delays can affect customer
satisfaction, operational efficiency, logistics costs, and supply
chain planning.

Historical order, shipping, customer, product, financial, and
geographical data are available. However, the company wants to
move beyond descriptive reporting and use data science to
identify factors associated with shipment delays and support
proactive logistics decision-making.

The objective of the proposed analytics project is to develop a
data-driven logistics analytics pipeline that measures logistics
performance, identifies shipment-delay patterns, predicts
shipment-delay risk, and generates actionable insights for
logistics planning and resource allocation.
